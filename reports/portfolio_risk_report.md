## Reading this page in a Capacity Delivery Review

1. Start from open high-risk items and the slip table - those are the only items that can still move a ready-for-service date.
2. Drift by supplier/category is a pattern view, not a blame view: a supplier drifting across categories is a capacity signal; one category drifting across suppliers is a design or submittal signal.
3. Every late item on this page carries a decision, not just a status: expedite, partial ship, resequence, or escalate - see the playbook.
